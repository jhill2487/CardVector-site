How approvals work

Enter or inspect a candidate path only when it points to a card image already in CardVector's Cloudflare R2 archive. Use Preview before approving. You can mark a card for later review, reject an incorrect candidate, or keep it missing. Approvals are recorded in the private review table and synchronized to the private catalog. No public search change occurs. For entire missing sets, use external sources to research artwork and licensing rather than approving cards without verifying identity and finish.
