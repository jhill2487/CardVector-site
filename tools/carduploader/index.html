Why Putnam Collectibles Uses CardUploader

CardUploader has become one of the cleaner options for sellers who need fast card identification, standardized listings, and repeatable inventory workflows without fighting a monthly scan cap.

What makes it useful

For a small seller, the practical win is simple: CardUploader helps turn physical cards into organized listing data quickly. That matters when you are processing batches, keeping locations straight, and trying to avoid manual listing mistakes.

CardUploader currently lists its Unlimited plan at $9.99/month with unlimited card processing, subject to its fair use policy. That makes it easier to plan around than tools that limit scans per month or charge separately for key workflow features.

How it fits the Putnam Collectibles workflow

CardVector focuses on capture buffering, operator workflows, market review, and business automation. CardUploader handles the heavy work of card identification and listing creation. Together, that keeps the process focused: capture the cards, identify them reliably, then review pricing and inventory decisions with better evidence.
